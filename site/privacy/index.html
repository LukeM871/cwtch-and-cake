Privacy

What we keep, and for how long

Short version: we only store what we need to run a booking or show a review, we delete it on a timer, and there's no tracking at all.

WhatWhyHow long
Booking: your name, email and any notesTo hold your place, and so only you can cancel itDeleted automatically 30 days after the booking date
Review: rating, comment, display name and course yearTo show it on the site once it's been approvedUntil we remove it (just ask)
The email on a "Verified visit" reviewOnly to check the booking at that momentNever stored with the review
Server logsTo spot and fix problems. They never contain names, emails or what you typed14 days
Your colour choice (light, dark or auto)Kept in your own browser so the site remembers itUntil you change it or clear your browser

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, adverts or tracking scripts. The only other website your browser ever talks to is the admin login page, and that's only for the café owner.

Who can see your details

Only the café owner, through a login protected by a password and an authenticator app. Your email is never shown on the site, and reviews only show the name you choose.

Your rights

You can cancel a booking any time before it starts, which deletes it straight away. To have a review removed, or to ask what we hold about you, have a word with us at the till.
