Allergens and dietary info

Every item lists the allergens it contains (the UK's 14 allergens). Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten, milk, egg and soya, so we can't promise anything is completely free of them. If you've got an allergy, have a word with us at the till and we'll help you choose.

  • Vegetarian no meat or fish
  • Vegan no animal products
  • Vegan option vegan with oat, soy or almond milk
  • Gluten-free option made on gluten-free bread on request
